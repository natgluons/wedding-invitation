A JOURNEY OF TWO

Our Story

Ours is a love written across cities and time zones, and now, finally, we are counting down to the day we say “I do.”

  1. APRIL 2021

    How We Met

    We met for the first time, never imagining that such a simple encounter would be the beginning of our story.

  2. JUNE 1, 2023

    Our First Date

    Our first date was at Mall Kelapa Gading. What started as a simple meal turned into hours of conversation, and before we knew it, time had flown by.

  3. JUNE 2, 2025

    The Proposal

    After years of making memories together, Richard asked Natasha to marry him. With happy tears and a heart full of love, she said yes.

  4. 2025–2026

    Miles Apart, Still Together

    Natasha moved to London to pursue her studies at Imperial College London. Though thousands of miles apart, we stayed close through phone calls, everyday stories, and counting down the days until we could see each other again.

  5. JANUARY 17, 2027

    A New Chapter

    After everything we've shared and all the miles we've crossed, we're finally getting married. We can't wait to celebrate this special day with the family and friends we love.
